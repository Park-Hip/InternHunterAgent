# Direct-employer source availability - Vietnam

**Study status:** completed 2026-09-22.

**Method:** Low-volume desk research and manual page inspection only.

**Scope boundary:** No accounts, contacts, production collection, or scraping were performed.

**Evidence labels:** `V` means the original page was manually reopened on 2026-09-22.

`R` means an original employer/ATS page was reported by a parallel research lane on 2026-03-10 and was not reopened in this final spot check.

`R` findings establish a lead, not a current-posting assertion, and must be reopened before any use.

**Field shorthand:** `T/F/L/D/C/U` means title, full description, location, published or closing date, company, and original URL visibly available.

A missing `D` means that no date was visible in the inspected evidence, not that the role has no date elsewhere.

**Scoring:** Technical availability is 1-5, where 5 is an accessible original page with a full description and useful fields.

Access clarity is 1-5, where 1 is an express automation prohibition, 3 is no permission grant but a possible permission route, and 5 would require a verified compatible licence or explicit permission.

Public visibility, a robots file, and an accessible endpoint are not permission.

## 1. Executive answer

**Option 1 is technically available but only partially available as an operational source strategy.**

There is a meaningful technical cohort: 33 deduplicated Vietnam-relevant employers were found with accessible original career pages or official ATS boards, and six original AI-related pages were manually reopened.

The manually reopened pages show that full descriptions, original URLs, company and Vietnam location are commonly available, while publication dates are inconsistent.

The cohort spans direct sites plus at least six ATS families: Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Trakstar, and Workable.

It is **not legally approved** and must not proceed to automated collection without source-specific written permission or a clearly compatible authorised feed/licence.

Greenhouse and SmartRecruiters have express automated-access/data-mining restrictions in the reviewed terms, so they are excluded from an unapproved collection cohort.

## 5. Field-availability findings

The six manually inspected pages demonstrate that full job-description text and original URLs are readily available on original pages.

Vietnam location was visible for KiotViet, MISA, VNG, and VinAI.

Company identity was clear from the original page context for all six inspected examples.

A publication date was not reliably present.

Two directly inspected pages instead exposed a closing date, and only two had a visible future deadline.

A feasibility phase should treat published date as optional and record `date_type` separately as `published`, `closing`, `not shown`, or `unknown`.

Do not infer a published date from a search index, URL, or page copyright year.

## 6. Access/terms findings and unresolved permission risks

**Verified restrictions:**

- [Greenhouse User Agreement section 3(z)](https://my.greenhouse.io/users/agreement) prohibits automated means including spiders, robots, crawlers, or similar processes to access or use the services.
- [SmartRecruiters terms section 2.4.1(f)-(g)](https://www.smartrecruiters.com/legal/terms-and-conditions/) prohibit automated agents/scripts for automated searches, requests, queries, and stripping/mining data, and restrict harvesting of data-subject information without consent or legitimate basis.
- [FPT Digital terms](https://digital.fpt.com/en/terms-of-use) prohibit robots, spiders, automated devices, and manual collection for reuse without written permission.
- [Recruitee API terms](https://2688240.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/2688240/Legal%20Department/Recruitee%20API%20Terms.pdf) prohibit bulk copying, scraping, or export unless allowed in writing.

**Unresolved, not permission:**

- [Lever](https://www.lever.co/legal/terms-of-service), [Workable](https://www.workable.com/terms), and [Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/resources/terms) official materials reviewed are customer/service terms and do not grant InternHunterAgent authority to collect third-party public boards.
- VNG, MoMo, KiotViet, MISA, VinAI, CMC, Viettel, and VNPT pages were technically accessible, but this research found no compatible public collection licence.
- A contact or application address is an outreach route, not consent.
- `robots.txt` was not relied on and was not treated as legal or contractual permission.
- Any future processing must exclude applicant CVs, contact details, and other personal data unless a separately reviewed lawful basis, scope, retention policy, and permissions exist.

## 10. Research interpretation

This study supports direct-employer sources as a technically meaningful option: more than
10 Vietnam-relevant employers have accessible original career pages, several AI-related
pages are demonstrably current, useful fields are generally visible, and multiple source
families are present.

It does not establish authority for unapproved automation or select Option 1 as the project
strategy. The six manually validated direct-employer pages are ranked research candidates;
Greenhouse and SmartRecruiters have terms-based restrictions described above. No source
model is currently approved.
